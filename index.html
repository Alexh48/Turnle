Start on any square and drag through all of them.

How to play

Draw one line that passes through every open square exactly once. Move up, down, left or right, never diagonally. Hatched squares are blocked.

Every change of direction is a turn. Par is the fewest turns possible. You get three tries a day, and your best one counts.

Drag back to undo a step, or tap any square on your line to cut it back to there. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and Backspace.

Grids grow through the week: 5×5 on Monday and Tuesday, 6×6 Wednesday to Friday, 7×7 at the weekend.
