How to play

Draw one line that passes through every open square exactly once. Move up, down, left or right, never diagonally. Hatched squares are blocked.

Every change of direction is a turn. Par is the fewest turns possible. Finishing over par doesn't end the game: try again as many times as you like until you hit par. If you're stuck, you can give up and see a par line.

The clock starts when you place your first square and keeps running across all your tries. It only stops when you hit par or give up. Clearing the board or starting another try doesn't pause it, so a quick par beats a slow one.

Drag back to undo a step, or tap any square on your line to cut it back to there. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and Backspace.

Grids grow through the week: 5×5 on Monday and Tuesday, 6×6 Wednesday to Friday, 7×7 at the weekend.

Each grid has a difficulty rating from 1 (Gentle) to 5 (Fiendish). It's worked out before anyone plays, from how many different par lines exist, how many starting squares can reach par, how far a simple "go straight when you can" approach falls short of par, and the grid's size.
